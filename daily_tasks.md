## 🆘 If You're Behind

**No replies in 2 days?** → Change subject line and first sentence of email  
**No calls booked?** → Add urgency to proposal ("spot available this week only")  
**Calls not closing?** → Use `templates/discovery_call_script.md` objection section  
**Delivery falling behind?** → Prioritize top clients, communicate proactively
