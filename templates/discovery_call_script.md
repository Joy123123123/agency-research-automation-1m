# 📞 Discovery Call Script — Agency Research Automation

**Owner:** Md Jamil Islam  
**Call length:** 15–30 minutes  
**Goal:** Understand prospect's needs → book or close on the call

---

## Pre-Call Prep (5 minutes before)

- [ ] Research the company on their website and LinkedIn
- [ ] Check their industry, size, recent news
- [ ] Review their job postings (signals of pain points)
- [ ] Prepare 2–3 tailored questions
- [ ] Have your pricing/packages ready
- [ ] Open their email thread for context

---

## Call Structure

### Opening (2 minutes)

```
"Hi [NAME], thanks for making the time — I really appreciate it.

Quick agenda: I'll take about 2 minutes to share what I do, then I'd love to 
spend most of our time understanding what's going on at [COMPANY] and whether 
I can actually help. Sound good?

[PAUSE — let them respond]

Great. So just to give you quick context — I run Agency Research Automation. 
We help [INDUSTRY] companies automate their market research and lead generation 
so their teams can make faster decisions and spend less time on manual work.

But I don't want to just talk at you — I'd love to learn more about 
[COMPANY] first. Mind if I ask a few questions?"
```

---

### Discovery Questions (8–12 minutes)

Ask **3–5** of these. Listen more than you talk.

**Situation Questions:**
- "How does your team currently handle market research and competitive intelligence?"
- "Who on your team is responsible for lead generation right now?"
- "How much time would you estimate your team spends per week on research tasks?"

**Problem Questions:**
- "What are the biggest challenges you're running into with your current process?"
- "When you need a specific piece of market data, how long does it typically take to get it?"
- "How confident are you in the accuracy of your current lead data?"

**Implication Questions:**
- "If that research process was 3× faster and more accurate, how would that change things for your team?"
- "What deals or opportunities have you had to slow down on because of a lack of data?"
- "What's the cost of spending 20+ hours per week on something that could be automated?"

**Vision Questions:**
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would your ideal research process look like?"
- "What would need to be true for this to be a no-brainer for you?"

---

### Tailored Pitch (3–5 minutes)

*Only pitch AFTER you understand their situation. Customize to their specific answers.*

```
"Based on what you've shared, it sounds like [MIRROR THEIR PAIN POINT].

That's exactly the type of problem we solve for companies like [COMPANY].

Here's what we'd typically do for someone in your situation:

[OPTION 1] — [PACKAGE NAME]: $[PRICE]
What you'd get: [3 bullet points based on their needs]

[OPTION 2] — [PACKAGE NAME]: $[PRICE]  
What you'd get: [3 bullet points]

Most clients in [INDUSTRY] start with [RECOMMENDED OPTION] because [REASON].

Does either of those sound like it could be a fit?"
```

---

### Handling Objections

#### "It's too expensive."
```
"I totally get that. Let me ask — what would it be worth to your business if 
you saved [X hours] per week and improved your data quality? 

[Let them answer]

Most clients find that the ROI shows up within the first month. Would it help 
to see a sample report first before committing?"
```

#### "I need to think about it."
```
"Of course — this is a real business decision. What are the main things you'd 
want to think through?

[Let them answer — address each one]

Is there anything I can provide — like a sample report or case study — that 
would help you make the decision?"
```

#### "We're handling it in-house."
```
"That makes sense. What I've found is that most in-house research takes 2–3× 
longer than expected and often costs more when you factor in staff time.

Would you be open to running one parallel project — just to compare the speed 
and quality? That way you can make an informed decision based on real results."
```

#### "We don't have budget right now."
```
"I hear you — budgets are always a challenge. Two questions:

1. Is this a hard budget freeze, or is it more about prioritization?
2. If you saw a clear ROI, would there be a way to find the budget?

[If yes] — Let me send you a sample report this week. If the quality is there, 
maybe we can talk about timing."
```

#### "I need to check with [BOSS/TEAM]."
```
"Totally makes sense — who else would be involved in this decision?

[Learn their name/role]

Would it make sense to set up a quick call with them as well? I'm happy to 
walk through everything again, or I can prepare a short one-pager you can 
share with them."
```

---

### Close (2–3 minutes)

**If they're ready to proceed:**
```
"Great — I think this is a good fit. To get started, I'd need a 50% deposit 
of $[AMOUNT], and I can begin work immediately. Does [PAYMENT METHOD] work for you?

[Confirm scope, deadline, and next steps]

I'll send over a simple agreement and invoice within the hour."
```

**If they need more time:**
```
"Totally understand. Let me send you [SAMPLE REPORT / CASE STUDY / PROPOSAL] 
by end of day today so you have something concrete to review.

Can we set a follow-up call for [DATE/TIME]? That way you'll have had a chance 
to look at everything and we can finalize details."
```

**If it's not a fit:**
```
"I appreciate your honesty — I'd rather spend your time well than push something 
that doesn't fit. 

If things change on your end, please reach out — I'm usually at capacity but 
always happy to connect.

Is there anyone in your network who might benefit from what I do? I work on 
referrals and would appreciate the introduction."
```

## Key Notes

**Listen > Talk** — Your job is to understand their pain, not to pitch.  
**Mirror language** — Use the words they used to describe their problem.  
**One ask at a time** — Don't give them three options at once; present the recommended one.  
**Silence is power** — After asking a question, wait. Don't fill the silence.  
**Next step always** — End every call with a concrete next step scheduled.

---

*Remember: You're not selling research — you're selling saved time and better decisions.*
