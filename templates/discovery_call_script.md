# 📞 Discovery Call Script

*30-minute call framework to qualify prospects and close deals.*

---

## Pre-Call Preparation (10 min before)

- [ ] Research the agency: website, services, team size, LinkedIn
- [ ] Note 1-2 specific things about their work to mention
- [ ] Prepare a sample report or relevant case study
- [ ] Have calendar link ready for follow-up scheduling
- [ ] Review their lead score notes in `tracking/client_tracker.csv`

---

## Call Structure (30 minutes total)

---

### 🟢 Opening (2-3 minutes)

**Goal:** Build rapport, set agenda, take control

```
"Hey [First Name], thanks for making time — really appreciate it.

[If you referred by someone:] "[Mutual contact] mentioned you, 
and after looking at [Agency Name]'s work, I was excited to connect.

[If cold outreach:] I've been following [Agency Name] for a bit —
[specific observation, e.g., "your case study on X caught my eye"].

Quick agenda for today: I want to spend about 15 minutes 
understanding what you're working on, then share what I do 
and see if it might be relevant. Sound good?"
```

**Why this works:** Establishes you as the interviewer, not the salesperson.

---

### 🔵 Discovery (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Understand their pain, budget, and decision process

#### Research & Operations Questions

```
"Walk me through your typical client research process — 
what does that look like right now?"
```

*Listen for: manual research, time-consuming, inconsistent quality*

```
"How many hours per week would you say your team spends 
on research and reporting tasks?"
```

*Listen for: any number over 5 hours = opportunity*

```
"Who on your team handles most of that research work? 
Is it you, or do you have a dedicated person?"
```

*Listen for: founder doing it = high pain, no dedicated person = bottleneck*

#### Growth & Challenges

```
"What's your biggest bottleneck right now when it comes to 
taking on new clients or scaling up?"
```

*Listen for: delivery capacity, research time, hiring costs*

```
"How are you currently handling [pain point they mentioned]? 
What have you tried?"
```

*Listen for: existing solutions, what hasn't worked, openness to new tools*

#### Budget & Decision Making

```
"If you found a solution that genuinely saved your team 
15+ hours per week — what would that be worth to your business?"
```

*Listen for: their own ROI framing — let them sell themselves*

```
"When you make decisions about tools or services like this, 
is that something you decide on your own, or do you involve 
others?"
```

*Listen for: sole decision-maker or needs partner/team buy-in*

```
"What would need to be true for you to feel confident 
moving forward with something like this?"
```

*Listen for: proof of concept, trial, pricing threshold, timeline*

---

### 🟡 Presentation (7 minutes)

**Goal:** Show the solution, demonstrate value, handle objections

#### Bridge from Discovery to Pitch

```
"So based on what you're telling me — [summarize their pain in their words] 
— that's exactly the problem I help agencies solve.

Here's how it works..."
```

#### Core Pitch (keep it tight — 3 minutes max)

```
"We provide Done-For-You research and automation specifically 
for marketing agencies.

What that means in practice:

Every month, you get [specific deliverables based on their needs]:
→ [Deliverable 1, e.g., competitor analysis reports]
→ [Deliverable 2, e.g., lead research packages]
→ [Deliverable 3, e.g., content research briefs]

All delivered within 48 hours, consistently.

For context — [Agency Name similar to theirs] was spending 
18 hours/week on research. After working with us for 30 days, 
that dropped to under 3 hours. [Adapt to actual case studies]

What I'd like to do is show you what one of our reports 
looks like — can I screen share for 2 minutes?"
```

*[Share screen — show the most relevant sample report]*

#### After Demo

```
"Does this match what you were picturing? What stands out to you?"
```

*Listen and reinforce what resonates*

---

### 🔴 Objection Handling

#### "It's too expensive"

```
"I totally get that. Let me ask — what are you currently 
paying for the time your team spends on this? 

[Let them calculate]

So that's $X/month in staff time. We're at $1,250/month 
and you'd be saving most of that time. Net, you'd be saving 
$X-$Y per month plus gaining capacity for more clients."
```

#### "I need to think about it"

```
"Of course — completely understand. Can I ask what 
specifically you need to think through? 

[Listen]

Is it the investment, the deliverables, or just timing?"
```

*Dig into the real objection*

#### "I need to talk to my partner/team"

```
"Totally. Quick question — if it was just your decision, 
is this something you'd want to move forward with?

[If yes:] Great — what would be the most helpful thing 
I can give you to share with them? I can prepare a one-page 
summary or jump on a quick call with both of you."
```

#### "We already have a VA/someone doing this"

```
"That makes sense — can I ask how that's going? 

[Listen]

What we often find is that agencies use us to handle 
the volume and consistency side, while their team member 
focuses on higher-value interpretation and client communication.

Would it be useful to compare what we could deliver versus 
what you're getting now?"
```

#### "I'm not sure about the quality"

```
"Completely fair — that's why I'd suggest we start with 
a free sample report for [Agency Name] before you commit 
to anything.

It takes me 2 hours to put together, and you'll see exactly 
what you'd be getting. Sound fair?"
```

---

### 🟣 Close (3-5 minutes)

**Goal:** Get a clear yes, no, or next step

```
"Based on everything we talked about — does this feel like 
it could be a fit for [Agency Name]?"
```

**If yes:**
```
"Great. I'd suggest starting with our Growth plan at $1,250/month 
— it's our most popular and covers [X deliverables/month].

Since we're talking today, I can offer you the pilot rate 
of $500 for the first month so you can experience it with 
no risk before committing to the standard price.

Can we move forward today? I'll send you an invoice and 
onboarding form right after the call."
```

**If hesitant:**
```
"What if we started with just a free sample report this week 
— no payment, no commitment — and let the work speak for itself?

If you love it, we go from there. If not, no hard feelings."
```

**If not ready:**
```
"No problem at all. When would be the right time to revisit this?

I'll follow up then — and in the meantime, I'll send over 
a sample report so you have something concrete to look at."
```

## 📊 Call Quality Checklist

After each call, rate these (1-5):
- [ ] Did you get clear on their #1 pain point?
- [ ] Did they name a budget range?
- [ ] Did you understand their decision process?
- [ ] Did you show relevant social proof?
- [ ] Did you get a clear next step?

**Score 4-5 = strong pipeline. Score <3 = revisit your discovery questions.**
