# 📞 Discovery Call Script

**Creator:** Md Jamil Islam  
**Use:** Follow this script on every discovery call with a potential client

---

## Before the Call (5 min prep)

- [ ] Research the agency's website and services
- [ ] Check their LinkedIn and recent posts
- [ ] Review their client types and niche
- [ ] Prepare your screen share (pitch deck ready)

---

## Call Opening (2 min)

**You say:**

> "Hi {First Name}, thanks so much for taking the time today!
>
> Just to set expectations — this call is about 15 minutes. I want to learn about your agency, share a bit about what I do, and see if there's a good fit. Does that work?"

*(Wait for confirmation)*

---

## Discovery Questions (5-7 min)

Ask 3-4 of these:

1. **"Can you tell me a bit about your agency — what types of clients do you typically work with?"**

2. **"How many hours a week would you say your team spends on research, reporting, or data tasks?"**

3. **"What does your current research/reporting process look like?"**

4. **"What's the biggest bottleneck right now when it comes to scaling?"**

5. **"If you could wave a magic wand and fix one operational problem, what would it be?"**

> **Listen actively. Take notes. Don't pitch yet.**

---

## Transition to Pitch (1 min)

**You say:**

> "That's really helpful context. It sounds like {pain point they mentioned} is a real challenge.
>
> Can I share what I do and you can tell me if it sounds relevant?"

*(Wait for yes)*

---

## Pitch (3-4 min)

**You say:**

> "I help marketing agencies automate their research and reporting workflows. Specifically:
>
> - Weekly competitor research reports — automated
> - Lead enrichment — we enrich 100+ leads/week for you
> - Client-ready dashboards — delivered every Monday
>
> The result? Most agencies save 15-20 hours/week, which they reinvest in client strategy and growth.
>
> Pricing starts at $1,000/month. Many clients see ROI within the first 30 days."

*(Show pitch deck if on video call)*

---

## Handle Objections

### "I need to think about it."
> "Of course! What's the main thing you'd want to think through? Maybe I can address it now."

### "It's too expensive."
> "I understand. What's a budget that would feel comfortable? I have flexible options starting at $500/month."

### "We already have someone doing this."
> "That's great! Are they saving you 15+ hours/week? If not, maybe we can complement what they do."

### "Can you send me more info?"
> "Absolutely! I'll send a proposal by end of day. What email should I send it to?"

---

## Close (1-2 min)

**You say:**

> "Based on what you've shared, I think we could definitely help {Agency Name} save significant time on research.
>
> I'd like to propose a **7-day free trial** — no risk, no commitment. You get your first research report within 5 days and you can decide if it's worth continuing.
>
> Does that sound fair?"

*(If yes → send onboarding form)*  
*(If not ready → book a follow-up)*

---

## After the Call (within 24 hours)

- [ ] Send thank-you email
- [ ] Attach custom proposal
- [ ] Follow up if no response in 48 hours
- [ ] Log outcome in `tracking/client_tracker.csv`
