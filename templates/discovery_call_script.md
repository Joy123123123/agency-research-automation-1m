# 📞 Discovery Call Script

**Author:** Md Jamil Islam
**Duration:** 15–30 minutes
**Goal:** Qualify lead, understand their pain, present solution, close pilot

---

## PRE-CALL PREP (5 min before)

- [ ] Review the prospect's agency website
- [ ] Note their main services and apparent client types
- [ ] Check their LinkedIn for team size and background
- [ ] Have sample report ready to screen-share
- [ ] Have pricing sheet open

---

## CALL FLOW

### Opening (2 min)

> "Hi {first_name}! Thanks so much for making time. I'm Md Jamil Islam — excited to connect.
>
> Quick agenda: I'll take about 5 minutes to understand {agency_name}'s situation, then I'll show you exactly what I do in about 10 minutes. Sound good?"

---

### Discovery Questions (10 min)

**Team & Operations:**
> "How big is your current team? Do you have anyone dedicated to research or competitor analysis?"

**Pain Point:**
> "How are you currently handling research for clients — is that done in-house, outsourced, or using tools?"

**Time Investment:**
> "Roughly how many hours per week does your team spend on research tasks?"

**Business Goals:**
> "What's the bottleneck right now in taking on more clients? Is capacity an issue?"

**Budget Signal:**
> "Are you currently spending on any research tools or VA help for this?"

---

### Presentation (10 min)

> "Based on what you've told me, here's exactly what I can do for {agency_name}..."

**Show:**
1. Sample research report (screen share)
2. How reports are delivered (Google Sheets / PDF / Email)
3. Turnaround time: 24–48 hours
4. What's included in each plan

**Key talking points:**
- "This takes 0 hours from your team"
- "Your clients get better deliverables faster"
- "You can take on 3–5 more clients without hiring"
- "Cost is a fraction of a part-time researcher"

---

### Objection Handling

**"This is too expensive"**
> "I understand. What would be a fair investment if this saved you 15 hours/week and let you take on 2 more clients? Let me show you the math..."

**"I need to think about it"**
> "Absolutely. What specific concerns would be helpful to address before you decide? I want to make sure you have everything you need."

**"We're doing this in-house"**
> "That makes sense. How much time is your team spending on it currently? Could those hours be better used on client strategy and growth?"

**"I'm not sure about quality"**
> "Completely fair — that's why I offer a free sample report. If it's not better than what you're doing now, there's zero obligation. Want me to create one for your top client niche this week?"

---

### Close (3–5 min)

> "Based on everything we've discussed, I think the [Starter/Growth] plan at $[price]/month would be the perfect fit for where {agency_name} is right now.
>
> To keep it risk-free, I suggest starting with a pilot project — one full report for $500 one-time. If you love it, we go monthly. If not, you keep the report and we part as friends.
>
> Does that sound fair?"

---

### Call Wrap-Up

> "Great! Here's what happens next:
> 1. I'll send you a quick email with the pilot details today
> 2. You share your top 3 client niches
> 3. I deliver your first report within 48 hours
>
> Any questions before I let you go?"
