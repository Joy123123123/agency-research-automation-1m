## Daily Outreach SOP (Minimum Viable Routine)

| Time        | Task                                              | Volume      |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| 8:00 AM     | Send cold emails (new batch)                     | 20–30 emails |
| 9:00 AM     | Send cold DMs (Facebook/Instagram/Nextdoor)      | 20–30 DMs   |
| 11:00 AM    | Reply to any responses from yesterday            | All replies |
| 2:00 PM     | Follow up with demo-watched leads (Template 4)   | All pending |
| 4:00 PM     | Log all activity in your pipeline Google Sheet   | All leads   |
| 5:00 PM     | Review pipeline — move statuses, set follow-ups  | 10 min      |

**Weekly goal:** 5 demos → 2 closes  
**Monthly goal:** 8–10 new clients × $500–$1,000 setup = $4k–$10k/month
