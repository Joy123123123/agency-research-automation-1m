## 🎨 Design Notes

### Slide Design Tips
- Use a clean, professional template (navy + white or dark gray + gold)
- Keep each slide to ONE key message
- Use visuals: charts, screenshots, icons
- Max 30 words per slide (text should support speech, not replace it)

### Recommended Tools
- **Canva:** Free, beautiful templates (canva.com)
- **Google Slides:** Easy sharing, collaboration
- **Beautiful.ai:** AI-powered pitch deck design

### Colors
- Primary: `#1E3A5F` (navy blue) or `#2D2D2D` (charcoal)
- Accent: `#F59E0B` (amber) or `#10B981` (green)
- Background: `#FFFFFF` (white) or `#F8F9FA` (light gray)
