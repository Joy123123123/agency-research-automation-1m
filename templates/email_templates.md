## ✍️ Personalization Checklist

Before sending each email, verify:
- [ ] First name is correct and spelled right
- [ ] Company name is correct
- [ ] Industry/niche is specific to them
- [ ] [SPECIFIC REASON] in Template 3 is genuinely specific
- [ ] No placeholder brackets remain `[LIKE THIS]`
- [ ] Your email signature is complete
- [ ] Subject line makes sense in context

---

## 📊 What to Track

| Metric | Good | Needs Work |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Open rate | > 40% | < 20% |
| Reply rate | > 5% | < 2% |
| Call booking rate | > 20% of replies | < 10% |
| Close rate | > 25% of calls | < 10% |

**Low open rate?** → Change subject line  
**Low reply rate?** → Change first sentence or call-to-action  
**Low close rate?** → Improve discovery call script

---

*Personalization beats volume. 20 great emails > 200 generic ones.*
