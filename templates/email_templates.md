# 📧 Email Templates

Complete email sequences for cold outreach, follow-ups, and onboarding.

---

## 📌 How to Use

1. Copy the template you need
2. Replace `[BRACKETS]` with specific information
3. Keep personalization natural — avoid sounding robotic
4. Test subject lines (A/B test 2 versions simultaneously)
5. Track replies in `tracking/metrics_tracker.csv`

---

## 🥶 Sequence 1: Cold Outreach (4-Email Sequence)

### Email 1 - Initial Outreach

**Subject Options (A/B test these):**
- `Quick question about [Agency Name]'s research process`
- `How much time does [Agency Name] spend on research?`
- `I analyzed [Agency Name]'s top 3 competitors`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

I came across [Agency Name] and was impressed by your [specific work — campaign, case study, or niche].

Quick question: how much time does your team currently spend on research and reporting each week?

Most agencies I speak with spend 15-20 hours/week on manual research — time that could go toward strategy and client work.

I run a Done-For-You research automation service specifically for marketing agencies. We handle:
✓ Competitor analysis reports
✓ Lead research packages (50-100 leads with full contact data)
✓ Industry trend reports
✓ Content research briefs

All delivered within 48 hours, on a monthly retainer.

I'd love to send you a free sample report for [Agency Name] — no strings attached.

Would that be valuable? Just reply "yes" and I'll put it together.

Best,
[Your Name]

P.S. Happy to hop on a quick 15-min call if you prefer.
```

---

### Email 2 - Follow-up (Send 3-4 days after Email 1)

**Subject:** `Re: Quick question about [Agency Name]'s research process`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

Just circling back on my email from a few days ago.

I know your inbox is busy, so I'll be direct: I help marketing agencies reclaim 15+ hours/week by automating their research and reporting workflows.

A client I worked with recently said:
"We went from spending 20 hours/week on research to under 2 hours — and the quality is better than what we were doing manually."

I've put together a sample competitor analysis for [Agency Name] — want me to send it over?

Takes 30 seconds to reply — just say "send it" and it's yours.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## ✏️ Customization Tips

1. **Always personalize** the first line with something specific about the agency
2. **Use their language** — mirror terms they use on their website
3. **Short = better** — aim for under 150 words for cold emails
4. **One CTA per email** — don't give multiple options
5. **Test subject lines** — run A/B tests, track open rates
6. **Follow up 3-4 times** — most replies come on Email 2-3
