# 📊 Business Model — Agency Research Automation

**Owner:** Md Jamil Islam  
**Model:** B2B Research & Lead Generation Agency  
**Niche:** Agency Research Automation

---

## 🏢 Business Overview

Agency Research Automation provides businesses with:
- Targeted market research reports
- Verified lead generation lists
- Automated outreach campaigns
- Ongoing market intelligence retainers

**Value proposition:** We save businesses 40–80 hours/week of manual research time by delivering accurate, actionable research faster and cheaper than an in-house team.

---

## 💰 Revenue Streams

### 1. One-Time Research Reports
- **Price:** $500 – $2,000 per report
- **Delivery:** 48–72 hours
- **Content:** Competitor analysis, market sizing, industry trends, customer persona research
- **Target client:** Startups, growing agencies, consulting firms

### 2. Lead Generation Lists
- **Price:** $300 – $1,000 per list
- **Delivery:** 24–48 hours
- **Content:** 100–500 verified, targeted leads with email, phone, LinkedIn
- **Target client:** Sales teams, e-commerce brands, B2B SaaS companies

### 3. Outreach Campaign Packages
- **Price:** $1,000 – $5,000/month
- **Includes:** Lead list + email sequences + follow-ups + tracking
- **Target client:** Agencies, recruiters, consultants

### 4. Monthly Research Retainer
- **Price:** $2,000 – $10,000/month
- **Includes:** Unlimited research requests, weekly reports, competitor tracking
- **Target client:** Marketing agencies, PE firms, large brands

## 📈 Growth Strategy

### Month 1 — Foundation ($5K–$10K)
- Land 5–10 one-time report clients
- Convert 2–3 to monthly retainers
- Build case studies and testimonials

### Month 2–3 — Scale ($20K–$50K/month)
- Hire 1–2 part-time researchers
- Automate 80% of data collection
- Launch referral program
- Add LinkedIn outreach to email campaigns

### Month 4–6 — Team ($50K–$100K/month)
- Full-time team of 3–5
- Standardized delivery processes
- 20+ retainer clients
- Launch premium tier ($10K+/month)

### Month 7–12 — Agency ($100K–$1M+/year)
- White-label partnerships
- Sub-agency model
- Speaking/thought leadership
- SaaS product potential
