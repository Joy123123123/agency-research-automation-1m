# 💼 Business Model - Agency Research Automation

## Executive Summary

A Done-For-You (DFY) research and automation service targeting marketing agencies.
Agencies spend 15-20 hours/week on research and reporting. We automate this for $1,000-$2,000/month.

---

## 🎯 Target Customer Profile

### Ideal Client
- **Type:** Digital marketing agencies (SEO, PPC, Social Media, Content)
- **Size:** 3-20 employees
- **Revenue:** $200K-$5M/year
- **Location:** US, UK, Canada, Australia, Western Europe
- **Pain:** Too much time on research, reporting, prospecting

### Customer Pain Points
1. Spending 15-20 hrs/week on manual research
2. Inconsistent reporting quality
3. Can't scale without hiring expensive staff
4. Research bottleneck limits client capacity
5. Wasting founder/senior staff time on data gathering

### Why They Buy
- Save 15+ hours/week per client
- Get consistent, professional reports
- Scale without hiring
- Focus on strategy, not research
- ROI: $1,250/month vs $3,000+/month for a VA

## 🛠️ Service Delivery Model

### What We Deliver

#### Research Reports (core product)
- Competitor analysis
- Industry trend reports
- Lead research packages (50-100 leads with full contact data)
- Content research briefs
- Market opportunity analysis

#### Automation Services
- Automated lead scraping and enrichment
- Automated reporting dashboards
- Email sequence automation
- Social media monitoring
- Keyword tracking and reporting

### Delivery Process

```
Client Onboarding (Week 1)
    ↓
Discovery Call → Research Brief → Setup Automation
    ↓
Monthly Delivery Cycle:
Week 1: Data gathering (automated)
Week 2: Report generation
Week 3: Quality review + delivery
Week 4: Client review + planning
    ↓
Monthly Check-in Call (30 min)
```

### Time Requirement Per Client

| Task | Time/Month |
|------|-----------|
| Initial setup | 3 hours (one-time) |
| Automated data collection | 0 hours |
| Report generation | 2 hours |
| Quality check | 1 hour |
| Client communication | 1 hour |
| **Total ongoing** | **4 hours/month** |

At $1,250/month ÷ 4 hours = **$312.50/hour effective rate**

## 📈 Growth Strategy

### Month 1: Foundation (5-10 clients)
- **Focus:** Systemize delivery, get testimonials
- **Outreach:** Cold email + LinkedIn (50 contacts/day)
- **Channels:** Cold outreach only
- **Goal:** Build case studies from pilot clients

### Month 2: Momentum (15-25 clients)
- **Focus:** Referrals from Month 1 clients
- **Outreach:** Add cold calling, expand email volume
- **Channels:** Cold outreach + referrals
- **Goal:** First $20K month

### Month 3: Scale (35-50 clients)
- **Focus:** Hire first VA to help with delivery
- **Outreach:** Add content marketing, LinkedIn posts
- **Channels:** All channels active
- **Goal:** Systematized delivery, first $40K month

### Month 4: Acceleration (70-100 clients)
- **Focus:** Sales team, partnerships with agencies
- **Outreach:** Agency partnerships, reseller program
- **Channels:** Outbound + inbound + partnerships
- **Goal:** $100K month

## 📣 Marketing Channels

### Primary (Month 1-2)
1. **Cold Email** - 50-100 emails/day
   - Tool: lemlist or Instantly.ai
   - Templates: `templates/email_templates.md`
   - Target: 5-10% reply rate, 1-2% conversion

2. **LinkedIn Outreach** - 25-50 connections/day
   - Target: Agency owners, marketing directors
   - Message sequence: Connect → Value → Pitch
   - Expected: 20% acceptance, 5% conversion to call

### Secondary (Month 2-3)
3. **Cold Calling** - 20-30 calls/day
   - Script: Use discovery call script as base
   - Expected: 5-10% book rate

4. **Content Marketing**
   - LinkedIn posts: 1/day about agency efficiency
   - Twitter: Industry insights
   - Expected: Inbound leads by Month 3

### Partnership (Month 3-4)
5. **Agency Partnerships**
   - Partner with larger agencies as white-label provider
   - Revenue share: 20-30%
   - Expected: 10-20 clients from partnerships
