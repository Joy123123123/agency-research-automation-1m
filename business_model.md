# 📊 Business Model – Agency Research Automation

**Author:** Md Jamil Islam
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 🎯 Core Concept

Provide **Done-For-You Research + Automation** services to marketing agencies, saving them 10–20 hours per week and helping them win more clients.

---

## 💼 Service Offering

### Primary Service: Agency Research Package
- **Price:** $1,000–$2,000/month per client
- **Deliverables:**
  - 4–8 competitor research reports/month
  - Lead enrichment data
  - Industry trend summaries
  - Automated weekly reports via Google Sheets
- **Time to deliver:** 2–3 hours/client/week (automated)

## 🎪 Target Market

### Ideal Client Profile
- Marketing agency with 2–20 employees
- Located in US, UK, Canada, or Western Europe
- Offers SEO, PPC, content, or social media services
- Monthly revenue: $20K–$500K
- Pain point: Time spent on manual research

### Where to Find Them
- LinkedIn (agency owners, marketing directors)
- Clutch.co (agency directories)
- G2 and Capterra (reviews)
- Google Search (local agencies)
- Agency Facebook groups

---

## 🔄 Client Acquisition Process

```
Outreach → Discovery Call → Proposal → Pilot ($500–$1K) → Retainer ($1K–$2K/mo)
```

1. **Cold outreach** (email + LinkedIn)
2. **Discovery call** (15–30 min)
3. **Free sample report** (proof of value)
4. **Pilot project** ($500–$1,000 one-time)
5. **Monthly retainer** ($1,000–$2,000/month)

## 🛠️ Operations

### Tools Used
- **Research:** Perplexity AI, ChatGPT, Ahrefs (free tier)
- **Automation:** Python scripts, GitHub Actions, Zapier
- **Delivery:** Google Sheets, PDF reports, Email
- **CRM:** Google Sheets (client tracking)
- **Communication:** Email, Slack, Zoom

### Time Estimate Per Client
- Initial setup: 2 hours
- Weekly delivery: 2–3 hours (automated)
- Client communication: 1 hour/week
- **Total: ~4 hours/week per client**

---

## 🔑 Competitive Advantage

1. **Speed:** Reports delivered in 24–48 hours
2. **Automation:** 80% of work automated
3. **Price:** 50–70% cheaper than hiring in-house researcher
4. **Quality:** AI-enhanced, human-reviewed reports
